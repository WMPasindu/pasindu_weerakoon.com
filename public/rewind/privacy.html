Privacy Policy — Rewind

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Rewind is a puzzle game published by Pasindu Weerakoon. This policy explains

what the app collects and why. It is short because the app does very little.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. Rewind has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own.

Your progress — which levels you have finished, your stars, your reward stars —

is stored only on your device, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What advertising collects

Rewind shows ads through Google AdMob. To do that, Google may collect and

process:

  • your device's advertising ID, used to select and measure ads;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system

version, and coarse location derived from your IP address;

  • ad interaction data, such as whether an ad was shown, watched, or tapped.

This data is collected by Google, not by us — we never see it, and we receive

only aggregate earnings reports that identify no individual.

Google's handling of this data is described in the

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

and in **How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our

services**: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy →

Ads. You may also opt out of ad personalisation there; the game will still

work and will show non-personalised ads instead.

  • Consent (EU/UK/Switzerland): on first launch the app shows Google's

consent form, where you choose whether ads may be personalised. You can

change that choice at any time from the same form.

  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app removes all local progress. There

is nothing stored elsewhere to delete.

Children

Rewind is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect

personal information from them. The app is rated for a general audience and is

not enrolled in the Google Play Families programme.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos,

precise location, audio, or any files from your device. The app requests no

runtime permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with

a new "last updated" date.
